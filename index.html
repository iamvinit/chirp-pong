How to Play Chirp Pong

Each phone is one half of the court. The top edge of your screen is a portal: the ball flies through it, your phone chirps an audio/ultrasonic 16-FSK packet across, and the ball bursts out of the top of the other phone.

Controls

Drag anywhere (or use Left/Right arrows) to move your paddle. Hit with the paddle's edge to angle your shot. Swipe sideways as you hit to add curve spin that continues on their screen. Flick upward into the ball to SMASH it.

Trick Gems

Knock the ball through a gem on your court to load a trick that triggers on their screen when the ball arrives:

🔥BLAZE: Arrives 45% faster, trailing fire.
👻PHANTOM: Turns invisible across the middle of their court.
🔱MIRAGE: Splits into three. Only one is real.
💣BOMB: A bomb falls alongside; stuns their paddle on contact.
🌀VORTEX: A black hole opens on their side and bends the ball.
🔄MIRROR: Their left/right paddle controls flip.
🐜SHRINK: Their paddle shrinks to half size.
Heat Multiplier

Long rallies heat up the court: 6+ hits = ×2 points, 12+ hits = ×3 points.

Aimed Bombs

Every return charges your bomb meter. When full, tap while the ball is away to chirp a bomb onto their screen.
